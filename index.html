What we're collecting

One drawing, one idea: a Bengali tradition — a festival, a food, a fabric, a face — carried a hundred years into the future, without losing what makes it recognisably ours.

No brief is too literal or too strange. A pandal built from light instead of bamboo. Ilish cooked by a robot hand, still in mustard gravy. A rickshaw that flies over the Hooghly. Draw it the way you actually imagine it, in whatever style you already draw in.

Open to anyone with a connection to Bengali culture — across West Bengal, Bangladesh, or the diaspora anywhere in the world. All ages, all skill levels, digital or a photo of a paper sketch.

  • A Kolkata tram, still rattling, still flying
  • Adda over cha, somewhere off-planet
